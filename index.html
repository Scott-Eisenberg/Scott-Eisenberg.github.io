About Me

Hello! My name is Scott Eisenberg, and I am a 2nd year Computer Science major at California Polytechnic State University, San Luis Obispo. I am passionate about becoming a Software Engineer, with particular interests in artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, and building impactful projects that others can use and benefit from. I’m excited to continue developing my technical skillset through hands-on projects and collaborative experiences.

On campus, I am the Corporate Liaison for the Cal Poly Security Education Club, where I connect with 100+ professionals across 50+ companies, building relationships and coordinating opportunities that directly benefit our members. I also help plan and execute 25+ annual events, including technical talks, corporate partnerships, and Capture the Flag (CTF) competitions, creating hands-on experiences for students to grow their skills in security. Acting as the spokesperson for the club has sharpened my professionalism, communication, and event-planning abilities while allowing me to advocate for student involvement in cybersecurity.

In my free time, I love going to the beach and surfing. I also enjoy going on hikes, spending time with friends, and listening to music
